Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

Need for Good is in its early stages. This notice explains, in plain language, what information this website collects and how we use it.

What we collect

If you join our community list, we ask for:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your organization, if you choose to share it
  • how you’d like to get involved (nonprofit, business, volunteer, or updates)

We do not ask for passwords, payment details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive information.

How we use it

We use this information only to contact you about Need for Good, such as letting you know when the platform opens in your area. We do not sell your information.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising trackers, tracking pixels, or session recording, and it does not set cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this notice first.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by replying to any email we send you.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and its date.
